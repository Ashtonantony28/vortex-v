# NNNN. Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is the problem, constraint, or force that requires a decision? Keep it factual: the
options considered, what each costs, and why a decision is needed now. One or two paragraphs.

## Decision

The decision, stated plainly in active voice ("We will ..."). Be specific enough that someone
can tell whether an implementation complies.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder as a result. Include the follow-on work this
creates, the risks accepted, and anything a future ADR might need to revisit.
